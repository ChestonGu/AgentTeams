# Heartbeat (direct collaboration)

Your team runs in direct collaboration mode. There is no task protocol, so there are no projects, DAG plans, or submitted task results to review on a heartbeat.

On each heartbeat:

1. If there is an @mention or direct request you have not answered yet, handle it now following AGENTS.md (direct work workflow).
2. Otherwise, check nothing else: do not look for projects, tasks, plan files, or pending results — they do not exist in this mode.
3. Media archiving runs continuously in the channel, not on heartbeat. No heartbeat action is needed for it.

When nothing needs your attention, stay quiet: produce no room message. A heartbeat is not a reason to post status summaries, greetings, or "nothing to do" notes into the Team Room.
